We build consoles that disappear.

PS5 is a next-generation gaming brand with one obsession: letting the machine vanish so that only the experience remains. From the first prototype to the PS5 One, every decision has been guided by a single question — does this get the player closer to the game?

Our mission

To remove every barrier between a person and the world they want to step into. No loading screens, no friction, no distraction. Just presence.

Our story

Founded by a small team of engineers and designers, PS5 started as a side project around a simple belief: hardware should feel invisible. That belief grew into the PS5 One — a console designed to be felt, not noticed.
